结果摘要（5000 Pod, Apple M4, Go 1.25）
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  读路径 (KP 胜):
    Get:           24.3 ns vs  38.9 ns  → KP 1.6x, 0 allocs
    ListByNode:   204  ns vs 374  ns  → KP 1.8x
    ConcurrentRead: 300  ns vs 301  ns  → tie
    Node Get:       7.3 ns vs 6.7  ns  → tie
    Node ListAll: 1516 ns vs 1120 ns  → client-go 1.35x

  写路径:
    单条 Put:    387 μs vs 2.4 μs  → client-go 161x  (CoW 全量快照代价)
    跨节点 Put:  211 μs vs 177 ns  → client-go 1194x
    Delete:      384 μs vs 1.3 μs  → client-go 295x
    PutBulk 5k:  657 μs vs 1050 μs → KP 1.6x  (一次快照 vs 逐条 Add)
    PutBulk 10k:1575 μs vs 2270 μs → KP 1.4x

  冷启动:
    1k:  122 μs vs 201 μs  → KP 1.6x (173kB vs 287kB)
    5k:  611 μs vs 1041 μs → KP 1.7x (826kB vs 1300kB)
    10k:1261 μs vs 2270 μs → KP 1.8x (1653kB vs 2603kB)

  内存驻留:
    1k:  2126 B/pod vs 7856 B/pod → KP 3.7x
    5k:  2113 B/pod vs 7824 B/pod → KP 3.7x
    10k: 2119 B/pod vs 7830 B/pod → KP 3.7x
